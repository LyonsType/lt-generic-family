# LT Generic Family

LT Generic is a clean, modern sans-serif typeface family built around clarity, versatility, and everyday usability. Designed with balanced proportions and straightforward geometry, it delivers a neutral yet approachable tone suitable for branding, interfaces, and general-purpose typography.

The design emphasizes strong readability through open counters, even stroke widths, and carefully constructed letterforms. Characters such as the double-storey “a,” compact “g,” and geometric uppercase set give LT Generic a contemporary appearance while maintaining familiarity and legibility across a wide range of sizes.

Its minimalist construction allows the typeface to seamlessly adapt between professional and creative environments. The family’s restrained personality makes it highly flexible without feeling sterile.


To extend its versatility, the sans cut is accompanied by two companion styles:

**LT Generic Serif** — a refined serif counterpart designed for long-form reading and classic typographic contrast.
**LT Generic Typewriter** — a monospace companion that introduces a mechanical, retro-functional character ideal for coding aesthetics, technical layouts, and experimental design work.

Overall, the LT Generic family forms a cohesive superfamily that balances modern simplicity with practical functionality.
